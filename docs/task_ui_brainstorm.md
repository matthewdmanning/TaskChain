# Task UI Brainstorm

## Smartphone Countdown Timer — 1–5 Minute Focus Bursts

Goal: maintain attention, motivation, and momentum during very short work intervals. Theme: cyberpunk / action sci-fi. The timer should feel energetic and immersive without compromising legibility or creating distracting visual noise.

## 1. Neon Core Reactor

**Best for:** intense focus, urgency, momentum.

- **Centerpiece:** A large glowing circular countdown timer styled like a reactor core charging down.
- **Visual style:** Black or charcoal background with electric cyan, magenta, and violet light bloom.
- **Countdown behavior:** The ring contracts smoothly as time decreases, with subtle pulse surges every few seconds.
- **Time display:** Huge digital numerals in the center, ultra-legible, with a faint holographic flicker.
- **Motivation layer:** Short command-style text beneath the timer, such as **LOCK IN**, **HOLD FOCUS**, or **FINAL PUSH**.
- **Momentum cues:** Micro-particles drift outward, light streaks move slowly, and the ring intensifies during the final 15 seconds.
- **Emotional effect:** Feels like stabilizing a power core under pressure.

## 2. Tactical HUD Countdown

**Best for:** clarity, control, performance mindset.

- **Centerpiece:** A clean sci-fi HUD screen with the countdown framed by angular targeting brackets.
- **Visual style:** Dark gunmetal interface with luminous teal and red accents; minimal but sharp.
- **Countdown behavior:** Segmented progress bars shrink with precise, mechanical motion.
- **Time display:** A central bold countdown with small secondary readouts such as focus mode, round length, and next interval.
- **Motivation layer:** System-status language such as **ATTENTION STABLE**, **MOMENTUM ACTIVE**, or **MISSION IN PROGRESS**.
- **Momentum cues:** Every 30 seconds, a subtle sweep-line scans the screen like a tactical display refresh.
- **Emotional effect:** Feels like operating inside a combat suit or starfighter interface.

## 3. Hyperdrive Sprint

**Best for:** energy, speed, short-burst motivation.

- **Centerpiece:** A vertical energy chamber or thruster-style timer that drains downward as time burns off.
- **Visual style:** Deep black with hot neon orange, blue, and pink; brighter and more kinetic than the other concepts.
- **Countdown behavior:** Animated energy drains rapidly, with small sparks and motion trails creating a sense of forward movement.
- **Time display:** Stacked digital numerals with bold sci-fi typography and very high contrast.
- **Motivation layer:** Aggressive short prompts such as **GO NOW**, **NO DRIFT**, or **FULL COMMIT**.
- **Momentum cues:** The interface subtly accelerates visually near the end, making the final seconds feel like a launch sequence.
- **Emotional effect:** Feels like engaging a hyperdrive burst for a short mission.

## Preferred Direction

**Neon Core Reactor** is the strongest overall concept. It is the most visually distinctive, maps naturally to a countdown, and supports urgency and momentum without needing excessive interface chrome.

The **Tactical HUD Countdown** is the strongest alternative when clarity and restraint matter more than spectacle. **Hyperdrive Sprint** is best when the timer should feel more kinetic and aggressive.
